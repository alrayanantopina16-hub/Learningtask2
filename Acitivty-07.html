Selected Dish: Fried Egg

Ingredients

  • Egg
  • Salt
  • Oil or Butter

Steps

  1. Heat oil or butter in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Crack the egg gently into the pan.
  3. Cook until the whites are set, season with salt, and serve.
